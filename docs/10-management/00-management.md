# People Management

People management is the discipline of **building and sustaining the team that delivers the work** — hiring the right people, growing them, giving them clarity and feedback, and keeping the team healthy enough to keep going. It is one half of an engineering manager's job; the other half allocates and sequences the work itself. A manager who optimizes only for delivery borrows capacity from this half and pays it back as attrition.

This chapter keeps the two halves apart, and then splits the people half again: the structure that changes rarely — who is accountable for what, and where in the lifecycle a person stands — from the practice that repeats every week.
